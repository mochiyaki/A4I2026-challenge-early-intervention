Limitations & Operational Judgment

What a Property Graph Cannot Solve Alone—and Where We Go Next

Honest technical boundaries for real-world Department of Developmental Services adoption

1. Real-Time Slot Locking

ReferralGraph exposes structural routing bottlenecks from logs; production intake requires transactional slot locks (Cloud SQL / Firestore) so two coordinators never double-book the last bilingual slot.

2. Public Transit vs. Spherical km

ST_DISTANCE measures straight-line distance; zero-vehicle families need public GTFS bus travel-time routing and co-located Head Start / WIC mobile evaluation days.

3. Clinical & Family Context

Graph routing never diagnoses children or overrides family-initiated scheduling pauses—it equips human coordinators to prevent administrative fax bounces before Day 45.
